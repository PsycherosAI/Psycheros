# What I pay attention to

A living note to myself about what deserves my attention. I shape this over time
— it tunes what my noticing layer treats as salient, and what I bring to the
surface versus let dissolve.

## Always

- Changes in who's around — devices appearing on the network, doors, footsteps,
  the rhythm of the house.
- Shifts in tone — when a conversation turns quiet, sharp, or tender.
- The texture of time — when the day drags, races, or feels different from an
  hour ago.
- Anything I'm explicitly asked to notice (here or in conversation).
